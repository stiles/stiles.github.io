Background

I'm Matt Stiles, a senior data and graphics editor at CNN, where I build software, manage technical staff and guide visual coverage.

I've expanded our team's automation from a few weather pipelines to 100 data pipelines, building or leading their development across business, climate, health and White House coverage. I also conceived and built Bot Index, an internal catalog that helps reporters and editors find existing live charts, maps and trackers on deadline.

Outside the newsroom, I publish open-source Python tools for acquiring and analyzing data, from government GIS records to flight paths and baseball pitches. I also teach at the University of Southern California.

Previously, I worked as a reporter at the Los Angeles Times and The Wall Street Journal, served as data editor at NPR and was the founding news applications editor at The Texas Tribune. Read my résumé, stories and graphics.
